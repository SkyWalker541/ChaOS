PICO-8 on ChaOS
===============

PICO-8 is Lexaloffle's fantasy console. ChaOS doesn't include it: if you
own it, you can play it here with your own copy.
Get it at https://www.lexaloffle.com/pico-8.php

1. Download the Raspberry Pi version from your Lexaloffle account
   (pico-8_<version>_raspi.zip).
2. Put it in this folder, as the zip. ChaOS takes out what it needs the
   next time it starts. Or unzip it yourself and copy these two files here:

       pico8_64
       pico8.dat

3. The PICO-8 shelf appears. Its first card is Splore, PICO-8's browser of
   carts people share, which needs Wi-Fi. Carts of your own (.p8 and
   .p8.png) go in Roms/PICO-8.

Buttons: B and A are PICO-8's O and X (Y and X, and L1 and R1, too).
START or MENU opens PICO-8's pause menu. Hold SELECT + START to quit.

Your settings and cart saves are kept in ChaOS's userdata and stay through
updates. Only the Raspberry Pi version works: the Windows, Mac and Linux
ones are made for other computers.
